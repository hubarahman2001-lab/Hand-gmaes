# Hand Games

Fruit Ninja, 2-player tennis and a jigsaw puzzle, played with your hands through the webcam (MediaPipe hand tracking).

**Play:** https://hubarahman2001-lab.github.io/Hand-gmaes/

- Tracks your thumb, index and ring fingertips.
- Puzzle: make a fist over a piece to grab it, open your hand to drop it.
- Works best on a laptop in Chrome or Edge. Allow camera access when asked.
- No camera? Use **Play with touch / mouse**.
