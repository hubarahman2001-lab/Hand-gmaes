# Hand Games

Fruit Ninja, tennis and a jigsaw puzzle, played with your hands through the webcam (MediaPipe hand tracking).

**Play:** https://hubarahman2001-lab.github.io/Hand-gmaes/

- **Fruit Ninja:** Classic (3 lives) or Arcade (60 seconds). Slice ❄️ for slow motion and ⭐ for double points.
- **Tennis:** 2 players, or you against the computer.
- **Puzzle:** 6, 12 or 24 pieces, with the kitten picture or your own photo (stays on your device). Make a fist over a piece to grab it, open your hand to drop it.
- Press buttons with a pinch 🤏, or hold a fingertip on them for a second.
- Sound effects, with a mute button at the top.
- Tracks your thumb, index and ring fingertips. Works best on a laptop in Chrome or Edge.
- No camera? Use **Play with touch / mouse**.
